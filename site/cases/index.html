Troubleshooting Cases

How a TAC engineer works a case: scope the problem, gather evidence, analyse packets and logs, prove the root cause and validate the fix.

About these cases

Every case on this page is fictional or fully sanitised. Hostnames, addresses, timestamps and outputs are constructed for illustration. No customer, employer or confidential data is published.

Every case follows the same structure

  1. 01 · Problem statement
  2. 02 · Environment
  3. 03 · Reported symptoms
  4. 04 · Initial observations
  5. 05 · Investigation steps
  6. 06 · Commands executed
  7. 07 · Evidence collected
  8. 08 · Packet / log analysis
  9. 09 · Root cause
  10. 10 · Corrective action
  11. 11 · Validation
  12. 12 · Technical lessons
