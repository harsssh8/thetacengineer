About The TAC Engineer

An independent technical learning and knowledge-sharing platform.

What this site is

The TAC Engineer is an independent technical learning and knowledge-sharing platform focused on networking, network security, application delivery and infrastructure troubleshooting.

The platform documents technical concepts, practical labs, troubleshooting methodologies and engineering projects. Content is written the way support engineers write knowledge base articles: precise symptoms, reproducible steps, evidence, and a clearly stated root cause.

Who writes it

The owner is a Network Security Engineer developing expertise towards Technical Support and Technical Assistance Center (TAC) engineering roles.

The site is a working record of that development: what was studied, what was built in the lab, and how problems were investigated.

How content is labelled

  • Labs carry an honest status: Planned In Progress or Completed.
  • Demonstration content is marked with a Demo badge and a notice on the page.
  • Case studies are fictional or sanitised. No customer, employer or confidential information is ever published.

Approach

Understand the problem. Analyse the evidence. Find the root cause. Every write-up follows the same investigation flow, from problem identification to validation.

Focus areas

  • Networking & protocols — TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP, TLS
  • Packet analysis with Wireshark and tcpdump
  • F5 BIG-IP LTM and Advanced WAF
  • Linux, Nginx and infrastructure diagnostics
  • Cloud networking and network security
